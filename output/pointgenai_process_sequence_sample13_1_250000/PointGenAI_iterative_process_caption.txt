图名：PointGenAI 点符号综合迭代优化过程示意图
English title: Iterative optimization process of PointGenAI for point symbol generalization

图注：
图X展示了 PointGenAI 在同一样本区域内的点符号综合迭代优化过程。系统首先根据目标比例尺、点要素属性、空间分布特征和符号约束识别原始图面中的符号压盖与局部拥挤问题，并匹配重要点优先保留、比例尺驱动删减、密集区点筛选和结构保持等 Skills 生成第一轮综合结果。随后，VLM/质量评价从符号冲突、图面均衡、格局保持和符号表达合理性等方面对第一轮结果进行评价，并将残留热点过绘和局部失衡问题反馈至决策模块。智能体据此调整最小视觉间距、保留比例、局部位移和均衡约束，进一步调用邻近冲突检测、邻近冲突消解、均衡优化、符号压盖校核和结果反馈修正等 Skills，最终生成符号冲突更少、图面负载更均衡且主要空间格局得到保持的综合结果。本实验中，点数由 R0 的 904 个逐轮减少至 R1 的 232 个、R2 的 182 个和 R3 的 144 个；符号冲突点对由 802 降至 0，冲突消解率由 0.0000 提高至 1.0000；视觉可读性评分由 35 提高至 75；约束满足率由 0.4875 提高至最终 0.9313。

Caption:
Figure X illustrates the iterative optimization process of PointGenAI for point symbol generalization in the same sample area. The system first identifies symbol overlap, local congestion, and dense clusters from the target scale, point attributes, spatial distribution, and symbol constraints, then generates the first-round generalized result by matching Skills such as important-point retention, scale-driven reduction, dense-area point filtering, and structure preservation. VLM/quality evaluation then assesses symbol conflicts, visual balance, pattern preservation, and symbol validity, and feeds hotspot overdraw and local imbalance back to the decision module. The agent adjusts the minimum visual spacing, retention ratio, local displacement, and balance constraints, and further invokes proximity conflict detection, proximity conflict resolution, balance optimization, symbol-overlap checking, and result feedback correction. In this experiment, the point count decreases from 904 in R0 to 232 in R1, 182 in R2, and 144 in R3. Symbol conflict pairs decrease from 802 to 0, the conflict resolution rate increases from 0.0000 to 1.0000, the visual readability score increases from 35 to 75, and constraint satisfaction increases from 0.4875 to 0.9313.
